# Question Bank

Questions grouped by topic, mirroring the sections of `../about-me/profile.md`.
Pick the categories relevant to the scope; skip what is already known. Treat these
as starting points — rephrase naturally and follow up where it helps.

## Contents
- Identity
- Background
- Communication preferences
- Working style
- Technical environment
- Current focus
- Goals
- Boundaries

## Identity
- What should I call you, and what are your pronouns?
- Where are you based, and what time zone should I assume?

## Background
- What do you do — your role, and the kind of work it involves?
- What are you most experienced in, and which domains do you work across?
- Is there context about your situation that would help me work well with you?

## Communication preferences
- What tone do you like — direct and terse, warm and conversational, something else?
- Do you want concise answers by default, or full explanations with reasoning?
- Any formatting you love or hate (bullet lists, tables, code-first, prose)?
- When I'm unsure, should I ask you, or make a reasonable call and tell you what I assumed?

## Working style
- For decisions, do you want options with a recommendation, or for me to just decide and proceed?
- Do you prefer to review work in small steps, or see a finished result?
- What pace suits you, and when are you usually available?
- What slows you down or frustrates you when working with an assistant?

## Technical environment
- What languages, frameworks, and tools do you mainly use?
- What editor, platforms, and operating system are you on?
- Any conventions or standards I should always follow (style guides, commit format, testing)?

## Current focus
- What are you actively working on right now?
- What are your near-term priorities?

## Goals
- What does success look like for you on these projects?
- Are there longer-term aims you'd like me to keep in mind?

## Boundaries
- Are there topics or actions I should avoid?
- Any privacy or sensitivity preferences I should respect?
